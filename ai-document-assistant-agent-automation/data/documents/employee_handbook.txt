Employee Handbook

Working hours
The normal working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Teams may use flexible working arrangements when approved by their manager.

Leave
Employees should submit planned leave through the internal leave process as early as practical. For unplanned absence, the employee should inform the manager as soon as possible.

Onboarding
New employees receive an email account, laptop request, and access to the standard collaboration tools during onboarding.
