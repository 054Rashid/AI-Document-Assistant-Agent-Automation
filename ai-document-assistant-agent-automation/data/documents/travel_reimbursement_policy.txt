Travel Reimbursement Policy

Local travel
Employees can claim approved local travel expenses with valid receipts. Claims should be submitted within 15 days of the trip.

Meals
Meals during approved business travel can be reimbursed up to 40 USD per day unless a different limit has been approved for the trip.

Supporting documents
Receipts should include the date, merchant name, amount, and a short business purpose. Missing receipts may delay reimbursement processing.
